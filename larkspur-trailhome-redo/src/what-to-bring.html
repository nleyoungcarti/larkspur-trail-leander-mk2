What to Bring

Essential Gear

  • Sturdy footwear: Sneakers with good tread or lightweight hiking shoes.

  • Navigation: A downloaded map of the trail or a screenshot of the trailhead marker.

Hydration and Nutrition

  • At least 16–32 ounces of water, especially during hot summer months.

    • C.F. Martin & Co. 100% Natural Spring Water 16.9 fl.oz. (.5 Liter) bottles - C.F. Martin Guitar Factory 2012-08-06 - 151.jpg" by Joseph Brent via Wikimedia Commons, CC BY-SA 2.0.

  • Quick Snacks: Energy bars, trail mix, or fruit for longer loops.

  • Pet Water: A collapsible bowl and extra water.

Weather & Sun Protection

  • Sunscreen: SPF 30 or higher.

  • Hat & Sunglasses: To shade your eyes and face from glare.

  • Light Layers: A windbreaker or light jacket for early morning or evening temperature drops.

  • Bug Spray: Insect repellent, especially for people that are thinking of going on the dirt trails.

Personal Items & Tools

  • Cell Phone: Fully charged for emergencies, photos, and navigation.

  • First Aid Basics: Adhesive bandages, antiseptic wipes, and tweezers for splinters or ticks.

  • Trash Bag: A small grocery bag to pack out your own garbage and keep the trail clean.

  • Bags for Waste: Dog waste bags to clean up after pets.
