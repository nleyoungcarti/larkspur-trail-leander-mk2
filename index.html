Explore the Larkspur Trail

Image courtesy of MileStone Community Builders.

A scenic pedestrian and cycling path in Leander, Texas in the neighborhood of Larkspur.

View trail map

Quick trail stats

  • Distance: The distance of the trail is around 3.48 miles.

  • Difficulty: Easy, this is because the trail is paved (great for strollers, kids, and road bikes).

  • Route type: Out-and-Back

  • Pet policy: Allowed but must be kept by a leash.

Find trailheads, parking zones, and navigate via Google Maps.

  • Parking space/options: You may have a hard time finding a parking space because the main entrance does not have much space for extra parking.

  • A link to google maps.

Trail Amenities

  • Off road option: If you want a more natural hill country experience there are unpaved trails throughout the trail.

  • Views: The views at this trail are scenic and beautiful.

  • Rest stops: There are no rest stops throughout this trail.

    • Safety and Etiquette

    • Hours: Open from dawn till dusk (5:00 A.M - 10:00 P.M)

    • Remember to pick up after yourselves and your pets and bikers should yield to pedestrians.
